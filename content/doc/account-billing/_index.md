Signing up creates both an account and a first organisation called Personal Space, and every resource you deploy lives in one of them. The account identifies you: email or GitHub sign-up, two-factor authentication, and the SSH key authorising your Git deployments, which no other account may reuse. Members, roles, payment methods, invoices and notifications all belong to the organisation.
