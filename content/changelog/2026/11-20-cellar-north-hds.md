You can no longer create [Cellar](/doc/deploy/storage/cellar/) add-ons in the North HDS region (`fr-north-hds`). To store health data in Cellar, create your new add-ons in the Paris HDS region (`parhds`). The add-ons you already created in `fr-north-hds` keep working as before, you don't need to change anything.

- [Learn more about health data hosting](/doc/manage/regions/#health-data-hosting)
