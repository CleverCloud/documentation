These guides connect your applications through private networks, and protect them: they keep them out of public reach, and control the requests that get to them. For the security features of the platform, such as TLS or encryption at rest, see [Security & Compliance](/doc/security/). To link your applications, add-ons, and machines in a private network, see [Network Groups](/doc/network/network-groups/).
