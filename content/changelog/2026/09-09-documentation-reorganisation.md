The Clever Cloud documentation has been reorganized. The sidebar now follows what you do on the platform, from [Deploy](/doc/deploy/) and [Develop](/doc/develop/) to [Manage](/doc/manage/), [Network](/doc/network/) and [Security & Compliance](/doc/security/), and each section opens on a short introduction of what it covers. Pages moved accordingly, and every former URL keeps redirecting to its new location.

The [deployment guides](/guides/) have been reviewed along the way, and pages now cover topics the documentation left aside, such as the [Terraform provider](/doc/tools/terraform/), [Mise](/doc/develop/mise/), the [Otoroshi challenge](/doc/develop/request-flow/otoroshi-challenge/) or [driving the platform from an AI agent](/doc/ai-llms/).

A change of this size leaves things to adjust. If a page reads wrong, a link breaks, or a topic you need is still missing, [open an issue on the documentation repository](https://github.com/CleverCloud/documentation/issues): every page also carries an edit link, and contributions are welcome.
