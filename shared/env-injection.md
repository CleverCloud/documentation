## Environment injection

Clever Cloud injects the [environment variables](/doc/manage/environment-variables/) of your application into its environment, along with those of its [linked add-ons](/doc/manage/service-dependencies/).

### Custom build configurations

On Clever Cloud you can define some build configuration: like the app folder to deploy or the path to validate your application deployment is ready
To do that follow the [documentation here](/doc/manage/environment-variables/reference/#variables-you-can-define) and add the environment variable you need.
