Following the [new pricing of Keycloak as a Service](https://www.clever.cloud/blog/engineering/2026/10/06/keycloak-as-a-service-pricing-november-2026/), we now create every new Keycloak add-on with an XS Tiny Space PostgreSQL database, instead of an XXS Small Space one. The monthly price of the resources of a new Keycloak add-on is now €47 excl. VAT per 30 days.

Keycloak add-ons already deployed keep their PostgreSQL plan and their current price. You don't need to take any action.

- [Read the announcement of the new pricing](https://www.clever.cloud/blog/engineering/2026/10/06/keycloak-as-a-service-pricing-november-2026/)
