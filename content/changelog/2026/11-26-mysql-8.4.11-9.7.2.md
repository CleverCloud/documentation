We updated MySQL images for releases 8.4 (8.4.11-12) and 9.7 (9.7.2-3). They're now available for add-on creation and migration.

Both releases hold the InnoDB buffer pool locks for less time during page reads and concurrent range selects. `mysqldump`, used for backups and migrations, now dumps tables with compressed columns correctly, and compression dictionaries whose names hold quotes, spaces, reserved words, or non-ASCII characters, so a restore recreates them as they were.

MySQL 9.7.2 adds the `DISTANCE()` function, which computes the distance between two vectors with the `EUCLIDEAN`, `COSINE`, or `DOT` metric:

```sql
SELECT DISTANCE(STRING_TO_VECTOR('[1,2]'), STRING_TO_VECTOR('[4,6]'), 'EUCLIDEAN');
```

MySQL 5.7 and 8.0 are end-of-life and stay on 5.7.44-48 and 8.0.46-37. If you still run a 5.7 or 8.0 add-on, [migrate it](/doc/develop/database-migration/) to 8.4 or 9.7.
